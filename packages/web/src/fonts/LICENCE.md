# Fonts

The `.woff2` files here are downloaded from [Bunny Fonts](https://fonts.bunny.net) by
`scripts/fetch-offline-assets.mjs`, which is also where `fonts.css` is generated. They are the
three families the `wa-theme-glossy` theme asks for, and all three are under the
**SIL Open Font License 1.1** — <https://openfontlicense.org>.

| Family | Designer | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Figtree | Erik Kennedy | <https://github.com/erikdkennedy/figtree> |
| Chivo Mono | Omnibus-Type | <https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/Chivo> |
| Fraunces | Undercase Type — Phaedra Charles, Flavia Zimbardi | <https://github.com/undercasetype/Fraunces> |

The OFL permits redistribution of the fonts, bundled with software, provided they are not sold
on their own and the licence travels with them. Neither the reserved font names nor the files
are modified here: they are Bunny's subsetted builds, served verbatim.
